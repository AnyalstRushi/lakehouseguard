## Caveats (read these before quoting the numbers)

- The failure modes were written by the same author as the detectors. 100% detection means the platform catches the failures it was designed against; it is **not** a claim about unseen real-world failures.
- The agent's top-1 root cause is checked against the cause the chaos engine injected. It measures consistency of the playbook, not diagnostic skill on arbitrary incidents.
- Outlier recall is below 100% on purpose: some 400x outliers stay under the contract's `max` and pass the range rule. Distribution monitoring and a tighter per-category range are the remedy (see Roadmap).
- Throughput is the single-process pandas engine. The Spark example exists for volume; it is not part of these numbers.
